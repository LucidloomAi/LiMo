LiMo · design · on the PC

The lock screen and the time left display

What a child sees on the master PC, in the Paper and Ink theme. These are drawings to agree the look: the real ones are drawn by LiMo’s helper. Every picture opens at its real size, and the lock screen there can be used.

The lock screen

One card on the paper page. The left half says why the PC is locked and when it opens again. The right half is what can be done about it. The left half stays while a way out is open, so the reason is never out of sight.

Every reason

The picture and the words change with the reason. The stamp, the place of everything and the size of the card do not.

The ways out

Three, each only when the family’s rules allow it: ask a guardian, type a code a guardian reads out, or a guardian types the PIN. Digits can be typed or tapped. After five wrong tries the next one has to wait.

A second screen

Every screen attached to the PC is covered. Only the main one carries the card.

The time left display

While the PC is open, a small display floats above everything, by the clock. It is one line: the time left, and whether it is being counted. It can be dragged elsewhere.

Notices

A warning comes 15, 5 and 1 minute before a lock, and a notice when a blocked program is closed. They appear above the display and go away with OK, or by themselves.
