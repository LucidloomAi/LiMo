The icon set, in light clay

Every icon is drawn once, as plain shapes, and painted by one theme. Its colour says what it means: honey for time and LiMo’s own things, teal for allowed and at home, coral for locked and stop, and paper with ink for plain things.

At work

How the icons sit on a Paper and Ink screen, at the sizes a screen uses. The screens here are a sketch, not the product.

Tabs float, in glass

A tab bar is never fixed to the edge of the screen. It floats above the page as a strip of glass, and the page shows through it, softened.

Real sizes

Every icon at 24, the size of a row or a tab, then at 32, the size of a tile.
